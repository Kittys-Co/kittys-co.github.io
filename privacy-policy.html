Privacy Policy

Effective date: March 7, 2026

Kitty's Co provides software that helps users generate, review, and manage advertising creative for Meta platforms at the user's direction. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices available to users.

Information We Collect

When a user connects Meta accounts or uses the app, we may collect information needed to provide the service, including:

  • Account and business information made available through Meta APIs
  • Ad account identifiers, campaign, ad set, and ad metadata
  • Creative assets, prompts, copy, and other content the user asks us to generate or manage
  • Basic technical and usage information needed to operate, secure, and improve the app

How We Use Information

We use information to operate the app, generate and manage creative requested by the user, maintain integrations with Meta, support users, secure the service, and improve product performance.

How Information Is Shared

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that help us operate the app, when required by law, or as necessary to complete actions the user requests through Meta's platform.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Users may request deletion of stored data associated with their use of the app.

Data Deletion Requests

To request deletion of app data, email privacy@kittysco.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include the Meta account or business details needed to identify the request. We will review and process valid requests within a reasonable timeframe.

Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Third-Party Services

The app interacts with Meta and may rely on other service providers to function. Use of those services may also be governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to privacy@kittysco.com.
